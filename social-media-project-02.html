Project Overview

Purpose, content structure and design direction.

Objective

Demonstrate how a fitness brand can combine educational, motivational, community-focused and promotional content in a visually coordinated Instagram feed.

Visual Direction

A bold fitness aesthetic using dark backgrounds, bright lime accents, strong typography and a consistent visual system.

Content Mix

  • Brand awareness
  • Workout education
  • Motivational content
  • Community engagement
  • Promotional messaging

Intended Audience

Fitness beginners and gym-goers looking for workout guidance, training motivation and a fitness community.

Concept project disclosure: VOLT ATHLETICS is a fictional brand created for portfolio demonstration. This project showcases design and content-planning skills; it does not represent verified client work or claim actual engagement, reach or sales results.
