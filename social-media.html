A practical content planning concept designed to balance reach, useful fitness education, community interaction and trial-session enquiries throughout the week.

Duration 7 Days
Brand Volt Athletics
Project type Concept
Inside the project

From planning to performance tracking.

Five coordinated slides explain the strategy behind the weekly publishing plan, from defining the audience to writing captions and deciding which metrics to review. Together, they show the planning process rather than a collection of unrelated graphics.

Slide 01 / Project Overview

Content Calendar Overview

Introduces the project, the seven-day timeframe and the overall content planning approach.

Slide 02 / Strategy

Audience & Content Pillars

Defines the fictional fitness audience, primary goal, tone of voice and four content pillars: education, motivation, community and promotion.

Slide 03 / Weekly Plan

Seven-Day Publishing Calendar

Maps each day's format, topic, objective and call to action across Reels, carousels, static posts and Stories.

Slide 04 / Execution

Captions & Publishing Plan

Provides example captions, relevant hashtags, calls to action and suggested starting times to test against audience insights.

Slide 05 / Measurement

Performance Tracking Framework

Sets out a blank weekly tracker for reach, engaged accounts, saves, shares, profile visits and enquiries, alongside a deliverables checklist.

Consistent Publishing

A repeatable seven-day schedule with a planned mix of content formats.

Purposeful Content

Each planned post has an objective and a relevant next action for the audience.

Measurement Ready

A simple framework to record real performance data and improve future content.

Concept project disclosure: Volt Athletics is a fictional brand. This is a portfolio demonstration; no live campaign, client engagement or performance results are claimed.
