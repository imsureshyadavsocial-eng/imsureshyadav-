# MAISON FABLE — Brand Identity & Visual Guidelines

**Project type:** Fictional portfolio concept  
**Category:** Premium home fragrance, candles and lifestyle  
**Prepared by:** Suresh Yadav

MAISON FABLE is a fictional home-fragrance concept built around the role scent can play in everyday rituals. The proposed identity is quiet, tactile and editorial: a restrained serif wordmark, a warm ivory and charcoal base, muted terracotta accents, and supporting tones of taupe and soft beige. The visual language pairs generous whitespace with fine rules, subtle botanical linework and natural-material product imagery.

The concept is intended for design-conscious home-makers and gift-givers who value thoughtful objects, calm interiors and clear product storytelling. Its positioning hypothesis is premium but approachable, expressed through material honesty, considered packaging and small ritual-led narratives rather than loud decoration. The proposed type system pairs EB Garamond for expressive headlines with Inter for labels and supporting copy.

**Scope shown:** brand strategy, audience hypothesis, positioning, typographic logo concepts, color and typography systems, graphic elements, packaging and stationery applications, and sample social creative directions.

*This is a fictional portfolio exercise. MAISON FABLE is not presented as an actual client, and audience and positioning statements are strategic hypotheses rather than validated research.*
