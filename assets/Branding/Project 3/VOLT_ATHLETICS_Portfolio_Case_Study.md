# 30-Day Digital Marketing Campaign Plan — VOLT ATHLETICS

**Project type:** Fictional concept project for portfolio demonstration  
**Prepared by:** Suresh Yadav  
**Services demonstrated:** Digital marketing strategy, content planning, channel selection, creative direction and measurement design.

## Brief
Develop a practical 30-day campaign concept for a fictional fitness and active-lifestyle brand. The plan focuses on increasing relevant brand awareness and audience interaction, while creating a clear pathway for interested users to enquire about the brand's services.

## Strategy
The campaign platform, **MOVE WITH INTENT**, frames progress as a series of practical, repeatable next steps. Instagram is the primary channel, supported by email or website content where useful. The audience model includes two hypothetical segments: fitness beginners seeking confidence and regular gym-goers seeking structure and community.

## Deliverables
- 14-slide campaign strategy presentation and PDF
- 30-day calendar covering format, topic, objective and CTA
- Three creative concepts and five sample captions
- Audience engagement playbook and measurement framework
- Editable calendar and performance-tracking workbook

## Planning approach
The schedule uses 12 planned feed/Reel assets, 10 Story sets, 6 community touchpoints and two supporting content touches across 30 days. Activity targets are separated from performance outcomes. Metrics such as reach, saves, shares, profile visits, link clicks, DMs and qualified enquiries are defined for tracking, but no performance results are claimed.

## Measurement
The framework recommends weekly reviews and a day-30 closeout. Decisions are based on the account's own baseline and comparable content, rather than a single high- or low-performing post. Budget tiers are illustrative planning assumptions in INR, not actual spending.

## Fictional project disclaimer
VOLT ATHLETICS is a fictional brand created solely for portfolio demonstration. The project is a strategy concept, not evidence of a live campaign. It contains no claimed growth, ad performance, leads, revenue or testimonials.
